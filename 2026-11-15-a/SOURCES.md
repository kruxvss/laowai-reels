# Zhang Wuyi, Wuhan DIY submarines (research 2026-11-09)

## Solid (2+ sources)
- Laid off from a textile-machinery factory in 2008, in Wuhan, Hubei. Started building mini subs. (World of Chinese 2017; Alamy/agency caption 2016-10-28; Oddity Central 2012)
- Two-seat wrought-iron subs. Claimed: 30 m depth, 20 km/h, up to 10 h underwater. (Oddity Central 2012; World of Chinese 2017; Gizchina 2011)
- First buyer: a Dalian sea-cucumber businessman. Claimed 50 kg of sea cucumbers in 40 min. (Oddity Central names Cong Zhijie; World of Chinese)
- Grew to ~10 workers. (both)
- Manned subs did not pass the authorities' examinations, so he switched to unmanned remote-controlled subs. First unmanned sub finished 2015, tested in a Wuhan lake, utility-model patent. (agency caption 2016)

## Conflicting / avoid
- Price: $31,000 (Oddity Central) vs ¥100,000 ≈ $15,855 (World of Chinese). Do not state a number.
- Gizchina 2011 says a wealthy local ordered one "on the spot" at a Hubei lake test. Single source.
