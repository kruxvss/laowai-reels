He lost his factory job. So he built a submarine. 🟡🌊

Wuhan, 2008: laid-off machinery worker Zhang Wuyi started welding two-seat iron subs in his workshop. His first buyer was a sea-cucumber businessman from Dalian. When subs with people inside couldn't pass the official checks, he switched to remote-controlled ones and got a patent.

Depth, dive time and catch figures are his own claims, not independently verified.
