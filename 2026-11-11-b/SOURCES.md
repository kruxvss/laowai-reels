# Rescued, then married (Sichuan 2008 -> Changsha 2020 -> wedding 2025)

Animated recreation of a reported story.

## Documented
- May 12 2008, magnitude-8.0 Wenchuan earthquake. In Qingchuan county a two-storey residential block collapsed (Mothership).
- Liu Ximei, then 10, was pulled from the rubble by PLA soldier Liang Zhibin, then 22, after rescuers dug for hours (China Daily 2025-12-03; Mothership 2025-12-09).
- Her strongest memory: his dirt-covered hands, warm and protective (China Daily).
- They lost contact for more than a decade (China Daily; World Journal).
- 2020, Changsha (Hunan): Liu (22) was at dinner with her parents; her mother noticed a man at a nearby table who looked like "Soldier Liang". It was him (34); he didn't recognise her (China Daily, Mothership).
- He first saw her as a younger sister; the 12-year age gap worried him. She told him her feelings (China Daily).
- 2025: they married (Liu 27, Liang 39).
- Liang: "Saving people back then was my duty. Loving her now is from my heart." (China Daily)
- Story via Rednet.cn; 2.3 million views on Weibo.

## Notes
- SCMP's headline says she was 11; China Daily and Mothership say 10. We use 10.
- Romance began after the 2020 reunion, when she was 22.
