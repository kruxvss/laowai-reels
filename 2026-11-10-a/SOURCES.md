# The fake bank (Nanjing, 2013-15)

Animated recreation of a reported case.

## Documented
- Pukou district, Nanjing (Jiangsu). Opened 2013, ran about a year; shut by police January 2015 (SCMP, NPR).
- Looked like a real bank: counters, staff dressed as bank employees, security guards, computers, LED screens with interest and exchange rates.
- Called itself a "cooperative", not a bank; had no permit to act as a financial institution.
- Promised 2% interest per week (SCMP). Real bank deposit rates then were roughly 3% a year (PBoC 1-year benchmark ~3% in 2014).
- Nearly 200 depositors, almost 200 million yuan (about US$32-33 million).
- One depositor (Shan) received interest in advance (NPR / China National Radio).
- A Hangzhou businessman stopped receiving interest after four weeks, went to Nanjing, could not get his money back, reported it to police.
- Police detained four people. NPR: the money went into real-estate projects that failed.

## Derived
- "~104% a year" = 2% x 52 weeks, simple interest (our arithmetic, shown as approximate).

## Invented for the animation
- Layout, staff, the LED text, the exchange-rate numbers on the board, the street.
