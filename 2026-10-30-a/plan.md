# Reel 8 — "Said yes to one TV job" (Dashan) — scene plan

Script (VO, ~36s):
1. A Canadian student said yes to one TV job.
2. By morning, hundreds of millions of people knew his face.
3. It's 1988. Mark Rowswell is studying Chinese in Beijing.
4. A TV station wants a foreigner who speaks Mandarin. His professors give them his name.
5. Months later, he's doing a comedy skit, in Mandarin, on China's biggest night of television.
6. He wakes up a star. China calls him Dashan. Big Mountain.
7. He'd planned to stay a few years. He's still a household name decades later.
8. And it all started with one job he happened to hear about.
9. Most people never do. The jobs are out there. They're just scattered.
10. So we put every job and every agent in one place. laowai.ma

Facts: U of T Magazine, Maclean's, Wikipedia (Dashan), Government of Canada bio (2012). Audience 550M-800M in sources -> say "hundreds of millions".
