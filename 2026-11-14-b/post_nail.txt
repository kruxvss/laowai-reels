Wenling, Zhejiang, 2012: duck farmer Luo Baogen refused the compensation offer for his new five-storey home. The new road was built around it anyway. Photos went worldwide, and about a week later he signed and moved out.

China calls this a "nail house" (dīngzihù). Would you have moved?
