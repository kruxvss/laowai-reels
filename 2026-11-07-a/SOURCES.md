# Beijing Underground City (Dixia Cheng) — fact sheet

Documented (Wikipedia "Underground City (Beijing)", GlobalSecurity "Beijing civil air defense", Atlas Obscura):
- Built 1969-1979 on Mao's order after the March 1969 Sino-Soviet border clash (Zhenbao Island).
- Slogan of the era: "Dig tunnels deep, store grain everywhere" (深挖洞，广积粮).
- 300,000+ local citizens incl. school students dug; some sections without heavy machinery.
- 85 km2, 8-18 m deep, ~90 entrances mostly hidden in Qianmen shops, 2,300+ sealable ventilation shafts.
- Facilities: restaurants, clinics, schools, theatres, factories, roller rink, grain/oil stores, mushroom farm.
- Government claimed capacity for all 6 million residents; plan: half underground, half to the Western Hills. (Claim, not verified.)
- Opened to tourists 2000; closed for renovation since ~2008.
- Rumoured links to Zhongnanhai / Great Hall of the People: NEVER confirmed (no official disclosure).

Later use / modern:
- "Rat tribe": official estimate 281,000 people living in Beijing basements/shelters (2015, Wikipedia "Rat tribe", citing Shanghai Daily / Al Jazeera); 2010-11 ban on residential rentals of shelters.
- Civil air defence rules: air-defence basements required in new residential areas / civil buildings in key areas at 4-5% of floor area; "combining peacetime with wartime" use (GlobalSecurity).
- PLA "Underground Great Wall": CCTV documentary 24 Mar 2008 revealed the Second Artillery tunnel project; reported length ~5,000 km (3,107 miles) (Jamestown Foundation). Reported, not independently verified.
