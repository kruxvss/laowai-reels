# The bear in a suit (Hangzhou, 2023)

Animated recreation of a reported story.

## Documented
- Late July 2023: video of Angela, a sun bear at Hangzhou Zoo, standing upright; users claimed it was a person in a costume (wrinkled/loose skin, human-like posture) (CBS/AP, BBC via The Star, 2023-07/08).
- The zoo replied with a post written as the bear, saying critics "really don't understand me" (CBS News).
- Zoo: sun bears are small, "not all bears are behemoths"; a person could not wear such heavy fur in summer heat for long; "We are a government-operated zoo. There will never be situations like that." (The Star / BBC).
- Dr Ashleigh Marshall (Chester Zoo) to the BBC: definitely a real bear; loose folds let it turn in its skin and defend itself if grabbed.
- About 20,000 visitors a day afterwards, up ~30% (The Star).
- 2013: Luohe People's Park zoo (Henan) passed off a Tibetan mastiff as an "African lion"; it barked (National Geographic 2013-08-16).
- 2024: Taizhou Zoo (Jiangsu) showed "panda dogs": chow chows dyed black and white; the zoo admitted it (SCMP 2024-05).

## Invented for the animation
- Enclosure layout, visitors, the three online posts' wording (paraphrased claims), the zipper gag.
