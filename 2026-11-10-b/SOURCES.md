# The copy store (Kunming, 2011)

Animated recreation of a reported story. No Apple logo is drawn (plain glowing disc).

## Documented
- July 20 2011: American blogger "BirdAbroad", living in Kunming, posts photos of a near-flawless copy of an Apple Store (AppleInsider, Al Jazeera, Matador).
- Observed: a winding staircase, staff in shirts with the logo, name tags that only said "Staff" (no names), a sub-par paint job, shoddy build (AppleInsider, Matador).
- Staff "all genuinely think they work for Apple" (blogger, via AppleInsider).
- A nearby shop was called "Apple Stoer" (AppleInsider).
- Apple then had 4 genuine stores in China, all in Beijing and Shanghai (Al Jazeera).
- Officials inspected ~300 shops; ordered 2 copy stores shut for lacking business licences, not for IP (Al Jazeera).
- Aug 11 2011: 22 unauthorised Apple stores identified in Kunming (Reuters via Fortune).
- Metropolitan Times: the 5 self-branded stores first found sold genuine Apple products (via Al Jazeera). VO says "several of those stores".

## Invented for the animation
- Store layout, staff faces, the street, the officials' look, the CLOSED sign.
