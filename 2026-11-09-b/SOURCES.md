# Her finger and her eyelids (Nanning, 2020-21)

Animated recreation of a reported case. Names as reported (surnames only).

## Documented
- Huang, 28, Nanning (Guangxi). December 2020: visited his ex-girlfriend Dong, saying he had come to pay back money he had borrowed. She was ill and fell asleep.
- He pressed her finger to her phone to unlock it, then lifted her eyelids to pass the payment app's (Alipay) face-recognition check.
- Moved about 154,000 yuan to himself: her savings plus money borrowed on her credit. Took her phone and a coat and left.
- Motive reported as living costs and gambling debts.
- Next morning she saw the transfers on her other phone. Police arrested him in April 2021 in another city.
- Court: 3.5 years in prison plus a 20,000 yuan fine (reported by Nanning Evening News).

## Correction to the brief
- Not iris recognition: fingerprint unlock + face check.

## Not documented / invented for the animation
- Room layout, clothes, times on the phone screen, the order and number of transfers, the street. No split amounts are shown.
