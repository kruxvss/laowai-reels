# Lucky coins in the jet engine (Shanghai 2017, Anqing 2019)

Animated recreation of reported cases.

## Shanghai, 27 June 2017
- China Southern flight CZ380, Shanghai Pudong -> Guangzhou (A320). An 80-year-old passenger surnamed Qiu threw coins at the engine while boarding, "as a prayer for a safe flight" (police, via CNN/Xinhua).
- Other passengers alerted the crew. About 150 passengers were taken off.
- Nine coins found: one in the engine, eight on the ground. Total 1.7 yuan (~US$0.25).
- Scheduled 12:40; took off 18:16, more than five hours late. Police took her away; no damage reported (CNN, Fortune, Flight Safety Australia).

## Anqing, 17 February 2019
- Lu Chao, 28, on his first flight (Lucky Air, Anqing -> Kunming), threw two 1-yuan coins toward the engine for good luck. Flight cancelled, passengers deplaned, aircraft grounded for checks.
- Detained 10 days for disturbing public order. Yixiu District People's Court (Anqing) later ordered him to pay the airline 120,000 yuan (~US$17,200); the airline put its losses above 123,000 yuan (SCMP, 2020).
