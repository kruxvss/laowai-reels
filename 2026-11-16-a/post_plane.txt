He couldn't fly a plane. So he built one. Full size. ✈️

Kaiyuan, Liaoning: former farmer and welder Zhu Yue hit midlife and realised he'd never fly. "I couldn't buy one, but I could build one." Using a 1:80 toy Airbus and photos from the internet, he and five friends welded a full-size A320: about 37.6 m long, 60 tonnes of steel, more than 2.6 million yuan of his savings. Cockpit, spinning engines, boarding stairs. It will never fly, so his plan was a restaurant with 36 first-class seats and a red carpet.
