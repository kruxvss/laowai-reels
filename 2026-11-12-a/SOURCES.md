# Rather die on the road (Shandong -> 31 provinces, 2014-2026)

Animated recreation of a reported story.

## Documented (SCMP via The Star, 2026-08-18; original reporting Hongxing News; Xiaoxiang Morning Post)
- Liu Peijin, 69, former serviceman from Weifang, Shandong; 18 years in the military, much of it away from his wife, his primary-school classmate.
- 2010: complications from diabetes left her partially paralysed; no sensation on her left side.
- Repeated hospital stays failed to halt her decline; they agreed they would "rather die on the road than in a hospital bed".
- 2014: started touring in an ordinary car (Jiangsu, Zhejiang). Later a custom campervan: two beds, air conditioning, a sink, a dining table; stocked with medicines and nursing supplies.
- He calls himself the "driver, cook, doctor, nurse, and carer, all in one".
- Since 2014: all 31 provincial-level regions.
- Fujian: villagers helped when the vehicle lost control on a steep road. 2017: a Fujian hair-salon owner refused payment.
- April 2026, Xinjiang: tourist Cheng (23) filmed him feeding his wife; the video spread across Chinese social media.
- He says life on the road made her "100 times" more spirited. They plan another trip.
