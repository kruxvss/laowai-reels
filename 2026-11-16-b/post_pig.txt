This 26-storey tower isn't for people. It's for pigs. 🐷🏢

Ezhou, Hubei: a former cement company built two 26-storey "pig skyscrapers". Cargo lifts carry about 60 pigs at a time, and the towers can produce 1.2 million pigs a year with more than 800 staff. One vet graduate working there said he earned about 20,000 yuan a month. The catch: before work you shower, go through a 65°C drying room, and shower again (a nozzle even shampoos your hair), and staff lived on site for up to two months at a time.
