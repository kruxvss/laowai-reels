# The man who put himself on a wanted poster (Qinyuan county, Shanxi, Nov 2024)

## Facts used in the reel (as reported)
- A man surnamed Wang in Qinyuan county, Shanxi, made a fake "wanted" notice about himself and posted it online on 11 Nov 2024.
- The fake notice said he was wanted for extorting 30 million yuan and for having a submachine gun and 500 bullets, and offered a 30,000 yuan reward.
- The post was seen roughly 350,000 times within a day.
- Police found and detained him in under 24 hours. They found no weapons and no money: the crimes were invented.
- He told police he did it because he was bored.
- He was placed under criminal coercive measures (detained) for spreading false information.

## Fact vs claim
- The extortion, the gun and the bullets are claims ON HIS FAKE POSTER, shown as such ("His crimes?" then "No gun. No millions. Nothing.").
- The 350,000 view figure and the "bored" motive are as reported by the police notice via the press.
