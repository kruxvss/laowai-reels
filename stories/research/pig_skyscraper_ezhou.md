# The pig skyscraper (Ezhou, Hubei): research
Researched 2026-11-11.

## Facts and their sources
- Two 26-storey towers in Ezhou, Hubei, built by Hubei Zhongxin Kaiwei Modern Husbandry; first sows arrived Sept 2022; nicknamed "Bajie Buildings". Together they produce 1.2 million pigs a year; more than 800 employees. (SCMP, 2024)
- Owner company is a former cement company, which supplied money and cement; cost 2 billion yuan. (Jiemian/Yicai English)
- Cargo lifts each carry about 60 pigs up to the pig floors. (Jiemian)
- Senior technician Sun Kaineng, 30, a veterinary science graduate, earns 20,000 yuan a month; juniors with associate degrees 10,000; locals offered 7,000 even if unskilled. (Jiemian)
- Biosecurity: shower and pathogen test before shift; on entering the sties another shower, a 65°C heat-drying room, then another shower; a nozzle applies shampoo automatically so no one skips washing hair. (Jiemian)
- Workers stayed on site for up to two months at a time; employees "recently" began being allowed home once a week. Gym and billiard rooms in the building. (Jiemian)

## Script choices
- "A cement company builds two pig skyscrapers" (former cement company).
- "Over a million pigs a year" (1.2 million, SCMP 2024; Jiemian gave 600,000 for one tower at full capacity).
- Pay framed as reported: "A vet graduate working there said he earned about 20,000 yuan a month" (single reported case; on screen "A MONTH, HE SAID").
- "Staff lived on site for up to two months at a time" in past tense, since weekly home visits were later allowed.
- Calendar prop "60 DAYS" illustrates "up to two months".
