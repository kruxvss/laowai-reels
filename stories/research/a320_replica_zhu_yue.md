# The farmer who built an A320 (Kaiyuan, Liaoning, 2016-2018): research
Researched 2026-11-11.

## Facts and their sources
- Zhu Yue, from Kaiyuan, Liaoning; did not finish middle school; farmed onions and garlic, later a factory welder (AFP via Malay Mail, 2018-10-28). China Daily (2018-04-26): 40 years old; peasant farmer, then welder and motorbike mechanic, later ran businesses.
- Quote (AFP): "I hit midlife and realized I couldn't buy one, but I could build one."
- Guide: a toy/model A320 at 1:80 scale plus photos online (AFP; AvWeb). (China Daily: airplane models and technical drawings.)
- Five friends helped (China Daily: five friends; AFP: five fellow enthusiasts who work as labourers).
- Started October 2016 at a factory in a Kaiyuan industrial park (China Daily).
- Full size: 37.57 m long, 35.8 m wingspan, 12 m high (China Daily).
- By Oct 2018: 60 tonnes of steel and more than 2.6 million yuan of savings (AFP). (April 2018: 40 t and over 800,000 yuan, China Daily.)
- Features: simulated cockpit / replica flight instruments, rotating model engine turbines, a stair car for boarding (China Daily; AFP).
- It will not fly. Plan: a restaurant with 36 first-class seats instead of 156, and a red carpet so diners feel like heads of state (AFP).
- A real A320 costs about US$99 million (China Daily, 2018).
- Not used: officials told reporters a single aircraft cannot be a business premises (China Daily, April 2018); outcome after 2018 not found in English sources.

## Script choices
- "Around forty" from China Daily's age (40 in 2018) and his "midlife" quote.
- "Over two and a half million yuan" rounds down AFP's "more than 2.6 million".
- "A320 ... thirty-seven metres long" (37.57 m); on-screen "37.6 M".
- On-screen price tag "$99,000,000 / A NEW A320" from China Daily's figure.
