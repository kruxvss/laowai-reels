## 1. The fake wedding (Shanghai) - NEXT REEL
- Meng, 40, Shanghai; her small property agency failed in 2014.
- Met a driver, Jiang, while hitchhiking; asked him to stage a wedding (said parents pressured her). He was already married; wedding under a pseudonym.
- Told relatives Jiang was a big property developer with connections for cheap new flats.
- Bait: bought a ~1M yuan flat, sold it to her cousin at half price; cousin told relatives it was the connection.
- Showroom tours: "5,000 yuan/m2 off" (~20%). At least 5 relatives paid; some sold their own homes.
- Stalled for years; ~2018-19 rented flats and told relatives these were the flats they had bought. No property certificates ("temporarily impossible").
- Caught: one relative checked with the developer - she didn't own the flat she lived in. Cousin had spent 100,000+ yuan decorating a rented flat.
- Total ~12M yuan (US$1.6M). Sentences: Meng 12.5 yrs (contract fraud); Jiang 6 yrs (signed the leases); cousin 5 yrs (lied to relatives).
- Sources: Shanghai TV via SCMP (Jan-Feb 2025); The Star 2025-02-11; Malay Mail 2025-02-08; Gulf News.

## 2. Life sentence for highway tolls (Henan)
- Shi Jianfeng, Yuzhou, Henan. From May 2008 two sand/gravel trucks ran on fake military plates (military vehicles pass tolls free).
- ~2,361 toll passes in ~8 months; tolls evaded valued at 3.68M yuan. Jan 2011 Pingdingshan court: LIFE in prison. He didn't appeal. National outcry.
- Twist: the trucks were for his younger brother Shi Junfeng's company; Junfeng surrendered and said Jianfeng took the blame for him.
- Retrial (Dec 2011, Lushan County court): toll value re-assessed 492,375 yuan; Jianfeng 2.5 yrs; Junfeng 7 yrs; 2 plate-makers suspended sentences.
- Sources: China Daily 2011-01-11; Shanghai Daily (Shine) retrial report; AP/Washington Times 2011-01-14.

## 3. The sleeping ex-girlfriend's face (Nanning)
- CORRECTION to the brief: not iris. Face recognition + fingerprint.
- Huang, 28, Nanning. Dec 2020 visited ex-girlfriend Dong, saying he'd return money he borrowed. She was sick and fell asleep. He pressed her finger to the phone, lifted her eyelids to pass Alipay's face check, moved ~154,000 yuan (savings + credit) to himself. Took her phone and a coat.
- Money: living costs + gambling debts. She saw transfers on her other phone next morning. Arrested Apr 2021 in another city. 3.5 yrs + 20,000 yuan fine (Nanning Evening News).
- Sources: VICE 2021-12-15; MustShareNews; The New Paper.

## 4. The fake bank (Nanjing)
- Pukou district, Nanjing, ~2014: LED screens, counters, staff in bank uniforms; called itself a "cooperative", no licence.
- Promised 2% interest A WEEK. ~200 depositors, ~200M yuan in about a year. A Hangzhou businessman's interest stopped after 4 weeks; police detained 4.
- Sources: SCMP 2015-01 ("Looks just like the real thing"); NPR 2015-01-28; Shanghai Daily.
