# MOTION + SOUND SPEC - divyansh.fx 01 (frame-by-frame, 6fps sheets + audio transient analysis)
Rule: when owner says "inspo", copy ALL of this language (motion, transitions, icon style, glow, sound), different content/colours. Keep it glowy and alive, never flat.

## Global
- Canvas near-white. Always-alive background: (1) soft radial accent glow blobs that breathe behind the key word, (2) thin hairline arcs/circles drifting + slowly rotating in corners, (3) in pill scene giant blurred donut rings drifting. Nothing is ever static: camera push-in 4-6% per scene, floating elements bob.
- Text enters CHARACTER BY CHARACTER (typewriter) ~16-20 cps, each new char arrives blurred (r~8px), rises ~10px and sharpens in ~0.14s. First word of video: huge + motion-blurred, scales 2.2->1 and deblurs in 0.5s.
- Key word = accent colour sans or serif italic (Instrument Serif); the rest near-black grotesk (tight tracking).
- Floating "$" glyphs rise and fade around the key word + glow.
- SCENE TRANSITION = whip: last ~0.18s the whole scene slides left ~140px with horizontal motion blur growing and fades; next scene enters from right with blur settling over ~0.22s. Elements inside also enter blurred (scale 1.12->1).
