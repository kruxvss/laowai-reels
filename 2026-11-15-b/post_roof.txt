Beijing, 2007-2013: acupuncture-clinic boss Zhang Biqing spent six years building a two-storey villa hidden inside fake rocks, trees and bushes on top of his apartment block, about 800 m². Neighbours complained about damaged pipes and late-night parties for years. Then in August 2013 the photos went viral, officials gave him 15 days, and about 50 workers took it apart. He called it "just an ornamental garden".
